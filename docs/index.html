Assumptions, formulas & limits

This page is a scenario calculator. It does not use real-time market data, show a cap table filed with a regulator, or predict a future GNUS price. Click the ? beside any setting or chart to see a short explanation.

Inputs you supplied

  • Preferred equity: $50 million pre-money, $100 per preferred share, and 100 common shares on conversion. These are editable deal assumptions, not verified issued share counts.
  • POS: $20 billed per device/month; $15 assumed cash profit; the remaining $5 is mandatory use-it-or-lose-it GNUS AI credit.
  • Processing split: 10% tokens burned and a separate 10% of processing payouts paid to Genius Ventures wallets. The remaining 80% is available for other participants. This is a historical GNUS example, not a verified current on-chain rate.

Key limits and risks

  • Burn ≠ price increase. Token price can fall even if supply falls. The assumed market-cap path is a separate input, not a measured market response.
  • Use-it-or-lose-it ≠ automatic burn. The simulator assumes all $5 enters eligible processing. If credits expire unused, they are not necessarily burned on-chain.
  • GNUS paid to wallets ≠ company cash. The wallet chart treats tokens as held. Their USD value is unrealized and might not be saleable at the displayed price. At long horizons, full retention may exceed plausible token liquidity.
  • Resale lockout: Choose 0, 6, 12, or 18 months. No sales in months 1–N; first sale in month N+1. Missed income is not paid later.
  • Shares ≠ liquid income. Preferred equity may have special rights and may not be transferable. Warrant tokens require an exercise cost and may have restrictions or vesting.
  • Token model safeguards. Token-price projections stop if retained Genius Ventures GNUS would exceed all remaining tokens or if supply falls below 1% of its initial size. The 1% floor is a scenario guardrail, not a GNUS protocol rule. POS cash continues after this point.
  • Taxes, fees, operations and dilution are omitted. $15 is a requested contribution assumption, not independently verified net profit.

Sources

GNUS historical processing burn and commission; GNUS tokenomics notes. All other financial inputs come from the editable scenario and should be checked against the final offering terms.

Read before sharing this as an investment tool

Economics: This model treats the $5 credit as fully spent on eligible AI processing each month. If unused credit merely expires without processing, expiration alone does not burn tokens. A 10% burn and a separate 10% company commission leave 80% for node providers or other payouts in this illustrative split.

Price: This is a mechanical market-cap / supply model, not a forecast. Token projections stop when assumed retained Genius Ventures tokens exceed outstanding GNUS or remaining supply falls below 1% of its start. POS cash still runs across the full horizon.

Equity: This assumes a $50M pre-money valuation, new preferred shares issued at $100, conversion of one preferred share to 100 common shares, and equal per-share value for illustration. Actual rights, fully diluted capitalization, future financing, options and warrant terms can change the result.

Investor withdrawals: The tokens-per-month chart reports how many GNUS would be needed to reach the requested cash income. The separate balance chart limits withdrawals by the assumed number of exercisable tokens. Warrant exercise requires payment of the selected strike price, and restrictions, trading fees, liquidity and taxes are excluded.

Sources: Historical burn and commission description; GNUS tokenomics and current implementation caveats. Equity terms, $20/$15/$5 POS economics, and warrant quantities come from the scenario inputs, not an offering circular.
